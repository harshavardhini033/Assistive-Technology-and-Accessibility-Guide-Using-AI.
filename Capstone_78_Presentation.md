| Project Focus | Key Deliverables |

| Evaluating free AI accessibility tools. | Research & Verification Reports. |

## Slide 02 / 06 — Target Audience & Core Objectives

### Target Audience Groups

- **Students with Disabilities:** Requiring visual, auditory, or cognitive learning aids.
- **Faculty & Educators:** Need straightforward workflows for creating accessible media.
- **Campus IT Teams:** Responsible for software compliance and infrastructure.

### Project Objectives

- Benchmark 4+ free AI tools for accuracy.
- Establish WCAG 2.1 AA compliance steps.
- Automate alt-text generation workflows.
- Emphasize human-in-the-loop review.

---

## Slide 03 / 06 — Evaluated AI Tools & Assistive Technologies

### AI Tools Tested

- **ChatGPT / Gemini:** Image description and alt-text draft generation.
- **ElevenLabs:** High-fidelity, natural text-to-speech rendering.
- **Live Captions:** Real-time audio transcription during lectures.
- **NotebookLM:** Document summarization based on W3C WAI source files.

### Assistive Tech Stack

- **Screen Readers:** NVDA, VoiceOver integration.
- **Standards:** W3C WCAG 2.1 AA Guidelines.
- **Multi-Modal Access:** Combining audio, text, and visual captions.

---

## Slide 04 / 06 — Accuracy Findings & AI Hallucinations

### Accuracy Benchmark

- **Alt-Text Generation:** ~90% accuracy on standard images.
- **Audio Transcription:** ~85–90% accuracy in low-noise environments.
- **TTS Voice Output:** High reliability across academic prose.

### Identified Limitations

- **Technical Jargon:** Minor errors when parsing complex mathematical charts.
- **Hallucinations:** Inherent risk of fabricating visual details on low-contrast graphics.
- **Mitigation:** Mandatory human verification step.

---

## Slide 05 / 06 — Inclusive Content Checklist & Best Practices

### Content Checklist

- [x] Meaningful alt-text on all educational graphics.
- [x] Synchronized live captions on video lectures.
- [x] Contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text.
- [x] Strict H1–H3 heading structure for screen readers.

### Implementation Workflow

- **Drafting:** Use Python automation to generate alt-text prompts.
- **Review:** Educator verifies generated descriptions.
- **Publishing:** Distribute multi-modal materials across LMS.

---

## Slide 06 / 06 — Ethics & Human-in-the-Loop Conclusion

### Ethical Framework

- **Lived Experience Consultation:** Always test software with disabled users.
- **No Full Automation:** AI aids accessibility; it does not replace human evaluation.
- **Privacy Standards:** Protect student data during AI prompt processing.

### Final Takeaways

- AI drastically speeds up caption and alt-text creation.
- Responsible AI usage requires human review.
- Universal design benefits all campus learners.
